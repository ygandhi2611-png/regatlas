How It's Measuredclick to expand

A simple additive formula per product, starting at 100. Not an AI judgment, every point traces to a specific registration:

The numbers follow a severity scale, from mildly routine to actively urgent. A market waiting on a decision is normal and costs the least. A market you haven't even filed in yet is more concerning, since nothing is even in motion. A market about to lapse is the closest thing to an emergency here, so it costs the most.

The date-based penalty isn't a flat "90 days" rule. A flat threshold treats every market the same, but a renewal that needs 18 months of prep and review (ANVISA, NMPA) is already overdue for action long before one that only needs a few months (FDA). So instead of "days until expiry," this checks "days until you needed to have already started," using an authority-specific lead-time estimate.

  • −5 for each market marked Pending: routine, still under review
  • −12 for each market marked Not Filed: nothing submitted yet
  • −18 for each market marked Expiring: active registration about to lapse
  • −10 more if you're within 30 days of needing to start the renewal for that authority
  • −25 more if that action deadline has already passed, the worst case

Clamped between 0–100. Weights and lead times below are illustrative approximations, not officially published or regulator-issued figures; real review times vary case by case.

Illustrative total lead time (prep + review) by authority: FDA/EMA ~9mo · MHRA/HSA ~6mo · Health Canada/TGA ~8mo · MFDS ~11mo · CDSCO/COFEPRIS/SFDA ~12mo · TFDA ~12mo · PMDA ~16mo · ANVISA/NMPA ~18mo · unlisted authority ~6mo default.

A worked example

Say a product is registered in 4 countries: Approved in two, Pending in one, and Expiring in the fourth, an ANVISA registration with 300 days left. ANVISA's illustrative lead time is 18 months (~540 days), so the action deadline already passed 240 days ago, even though the license itself doesn't expire for another 300 days.

  • 0 for the two Approved markets, no penalty
  • −5 for the Pending market
  • −18 for the Expiring market
  • −25 more since its action deadline is already overdue

Total penalty: 5 + 18 + 25 = 48. Score: 100 − 48 = 52, still Watch, but far closer to At Risk than a flat 90-day rule would have shown for a license with 300 days left.

What the bands mean

Healthy · 70–100

Registrations largely approved, nothing expiring imminently. Routine monitoring is enough.

Watch · 50–69

Some pending or soon-to-expire markets. Worth a closer look before it becomes urgent.

At Risk · 0–49

Multiple not-filed, expiring, or past-due markets stacking up. This is what the Action Queue surfaces first.

How to Use

From registration data to portfolio clarity, in minutes

1
Add your registrations

Enter product, country, status, and expiry in the Register tab. No jargon required, just what you actually know about the product and market.

2
Explore by tab

Switch between Register, Map, Health, Expiry Calendar, and Timeline to see the same portfolio from different angles: geography, product risk, upcoming load, and lifecycle stage.

3
Watch the Action Queue

The panel on the right always shows what needs attention first: products flagged "At Risk" or "Watch," and Action Deadlines, no matter which tab you're on. Action Deadlines aren't just "days until expiry": they factor in how long that specific authority typically takes to review a renewal, so something can show as already overdue even if the license itself doesn't expire for months yet.

4
Export or reset anytime

Download your data as a CSV for your own records, or reset to the sample dataset to start over. Nothing here is sent anywhere.

Curious how the Health Score is actually calculated, and what "Healthy / Watch / At Risk" means? Open the Health tab and expand the "How It's Measured" panel underneath the scores.
