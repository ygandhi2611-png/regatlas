# RegAtlas

A regulatory portfolio register — a way to see every product registration, in every market, in one place.

🔗 **Try it here:** https://ygandhi2611-png.github.io/regatlas/

## Why this exists

Most small and mid-size regulatory teams are stuck between two bad options: an Excel or SharePoint tracker that gets messier every quarter, or an enterprise system like Veeva Vault that costs $500,000–$2,000,000+ a year. RegAtlas is built for the space in between — something that gives structure and visibility without the enterprise price tag.

## What you can do with it

- **See everything at a glance** — a full register of every product's registration status, across every market
- **Explore on a map** — a rotatable globe showing where products are registered, with click-through to details
- **Spot problems early** — a health score for each product/market that flags what needs attention
- **Track what's expiring** — a calendar view of upcoming renewal deadlines
- **Follow the history** — a timeline of each registration's lifecycle
- **Stay on top of urgent items** — an action list of what needs attention first, always visible
- **Export your data** — pull everything out as a CSV whenever you need it

It comes pre-loaded with 48 countries across six regions and realistic examples from major health authorities (FDA, EMA, MHRA, PMDA, Health Canada, TGA, CDSCO, ANVISA, COFEPRIS), so you can see it in action right away.

## What it's not

RegAtlas is not a compliance system — it doesn't meet 21 CFR Part 11 requirements, and it's not meant to replace an enterprise RIM platform. It's a lightweight visibility tool for teams who've outgrown spreadsheets but aren't ready for (or don't need) a seven-figure system. That's a deliberate choice, not a shortcoming.
